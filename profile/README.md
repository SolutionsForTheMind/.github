# NeuroAcoustics

**Solutions for the Mind** — AI-driven acoustic neuromodulation for sleep and memory.

NeuroAcoustics is an Argentine deep-tech start-up founded in 2022 by researchers from the Sleep and Memory Laboratory at ITBA (Instituto Tecnológico de Buenos Aires). We develop a wearable headband that records brain activity during sleep, detects slow-wave oscillations in real time, and delivers precisely timed, non-invasive sound stimulation tailored to each user.

> Our device is under development and has not been cleared or approved by the FDA or any other regulatory agency.

## Software transparency
